About PityHub

PityHub is a free, no-login tools hub for gacha games. It started with two games — Neverness to Everness (NTE) and Zenless Zone Zero (ZZZ) — and is built as a multi-game hub: more games will be added later, each with its own simulator, pity tracker, characters, items, banners, tier list, and codes.

What you can do here

  • Wish simulators — simulate NTE Scarborough Fair and ZZZ Signal Search pulls, with each game's real pity rules.
  • Pity tracker — enter your current pity and see your exact odds for the next pulls, with the cost in Annulith or Polychrome.
  • Damage calculator — a simple ATK / CRIT / DMG% estimator for both games (labelled as an estimator, not game-accurate damage).
  • Character and item databases — 26 NTE characters and 62 ZZZ agents, 103 ZZZ W-Engines, 30 Drive Disc sets, 41 Bangboo, and NTE Arcs — searchable and filterable.
  • Banner schedule, tier lists, and codes — current banners with dates, community tier snapshots, and active redeem codes.

Everything is free and works without an account. The only thing the site remembers is the pity number you choose to save in the Pity Tracker — in your own browser, never sent anywhere (see the Privacy Policy).

Data sources

Character and item data comes from community sources, checked against each other before publishing:

  • Enka.Network — ZZZ agent icons, portraits, and IDs.
  • Fandom ZZZ Wiki (CC BY-SA text) — ZZZ Bangboo images and reference data.
  • nte.wiki — NTE character icons.
  • Prydwen, Game8, TimeSaver, SenseOS, and HoYoLAB / HoYoverse notices — rates, banners, tier snapshots, and codes.

Character and item artwork is © HoYoverse / Hotta Studio & Perfect World, used here as small functional icons with attribution.

Honesty policy — what we do not know

We label uncertainty instead of hiding it:

  • NTE gacha rates are unverified. No official Hotta / Perfect World rate sheet was found. The base S rate is disputed — 0.6% in a CBT-era table vs 0.99% in the current community model — and this site uses the 0.99% model, labelled clearly wherever it appears.
  • Tier lists are community snapshots, not official rankings. HoYoverse and Hotta / Perfect World publish no official tier lists. The ZZZ list is a TimeSaver snapshot (1 Nov 2026); the NTE list is a v1.4 community consensus with no invented S+/S/A split.
  • Items marked TBD are genuinely unknown. Featured A-ranks for the 11 Nov banners, Severian's Disc set, and some NTE images are listed as "to be decided" until the official notices land — they are not guessed.
