Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 Nov 2026.

PityHub is a free, no-login tools site for Neverness to Everness (NTE) and Zenless Zone Zero (ZZZ). This policy says, in plain English, what the site does and does not collect. Short version: the site itself collects no personal data about you.

No accounts, no login, no cookies of our own

PityHub has no accounts, no sign-in, and no registration. The site sets no cookies of its own and runs no analytics tool. We do not ask for your name, email address, game account, or any other personal information, and there is no form on this site that sends information to us.

What is stored in your browser

One feature saves something — only in your own browser, on your own device, and only when you ask it to:

  • Pity Tracker (pity-tracker.html): when you click Save, the pity number you typed is stored in your browser's local storage under the key pity-nte or pity-zzz (one number per game). It is read back the next time you open the tracker so you do not have to re-type it. That number never leaves your browser — it is never sent to us or to anyone else.

Everything else is kept only while the page is open and is forgotten when you close it: simulator pulls, damage-calculator inputs, and search/filter choices are held in the page's memory, not stored anywhere. You can clear the saved pity number at any time by clearing your browser's site data for this site, or by saving a new number over it.

Third-party resources

The site loads images and fonts from third parties, and is hosted by a third party. Those services may log your visit (for example your IP address) under their own privacy policies — PityHub does not control that, and does not receive those logs:

  • Hosting — GitHub Pages: this site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visits as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Character images — Enka.Network (enka.network): ZZZ agent icons and portraits.
  • Bangboo images — Fandom ZZZ Wiki (static.wikia.nocookie.net): Bangboo icons.
  • NTE character images — nte.wiki: NTE character icons.

Clicking a link to any of these (or to any other external site, such as Prydwen, Game8, or a game wiki) takes you to that site under its own privacy policy.

Advertising

There are currently no ads on PityHub and no advertising or tracking cookies. Advertising (Google AdSense) is planned for later. When ads are added, this page will be updated to say what the ad provider collects before any ad is shown.

Children

PityHub is a general-audience tools site. Because the site collects no personal data, there is nothing for a parent to request or delete from us. If that changes, this page will be updated.

Changes to this policy

If the site starts collecting anything new — for example when advertising is added — this page will be updated and the date above will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or a correction to it, can be raised on the PityHub GitHub repository: github.com/babyboovn01-prog/nte-zzz-hub. There is no email address for this site.
